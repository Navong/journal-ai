## Entry 3: Morning Anxiety (English-Heavy)

Woke up at 6 AM with my heart racing. Another anxiety dream about missing an important presentation. 요즘 이런 꿈을 자주 꿔요. It's probably because of the upcoming investor meeting next month.

I tried the breathing exercises Dr. Kim recommended - 4 counts in, 7 counts hold, 8 counts out. 조금 도움이 됐어요. After about 10 minutes, my heart rate returned to normal.

Decided to journal before starting my day, hoping it would help process these feelings. I know rationally that I'm prepared for the meeting. The pitch deck is ready, my co-founder Michael and I have practiced multiple times, and the product metrics are strong. 그런데 왜 이렇게 불안한지 모르겠어요.

Maybe it's not really about the meeting. Maybe it's about proving to myself that I can do this. That leaving my stable job at Samsung to start this company wasn't a mistake. 부모님께서는 여전히 걱정하시지만, 나는 이 길이 맞다고 믿어요.

## Entry 5: Family Visit (Korean-Heavy)

오늘은 부모님 댁에 다녀왔어요. Mom made my favorite kimchi jjigae and it tasted like home. 어머니의 손맛은 정말 따라할 수가 없어요.

Dad asked about my job again. He still doesn't fully understand what I do as a UX designer, but I can tell he's trying. "컴퓨터로 그림 그리는 거 아니냐?" he asked. I explained that it's more about understanding people and solving their problems through design. He nodded, though I'm not sure he got it.

My younger brother Junho showed me his college application essays. 정말 잘 썼더라고요. He wants to study computer science at KAIST. I'm so proud of him. When did he grow up so much?

Before leaving, Mom packed me enough banchan to last two weeks. As I was putting on my shoes, she said "서울에서 잘 먹고 다녀야 해. 너무 말랐어." Mothers never change, do they? Even though I'm 28 now, I'll always be her child who needs to eat more.

The KTX ride back to Seoul gave me time to think. Family visits always leave me feeling both full and empty - full of love and good food, empty because I miss them the moment I leave. 더 자주 찾아뵐 수 있으면 좋을텐데.
